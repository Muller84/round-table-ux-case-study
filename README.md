# Round Table – Stonewake Keep (HCI & UX Case Study)

An inclusive mobile booking companion app designed for **Stonewake Keep**, a historic hotel in the Lake District, created as part of a Level 6 Software Engineering / HCI university module.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Declared Focus:** Focus A – The Keep Stay (General hotel booking, accessible room selection, and experience add-ons)[cite: 135, 140].
* **Methodology:** User-Centred Design (UCD), Heuristic Evaluation, Iterative Prototyping[cite: 138, 142].
* **Core Design Target:** Balancing historic building physical constraints (steep stairs, narrow doorways) with WCAG 2.1 accessibility and transparent pricing[cite: 135].

---

## 🎯 Key HCI Challenges & Strategic Solutions

1. **Inclusive Design over Exclusion:**
   * *Problem:* Commercial platforms often hide accessibility options deep within sub-menus[cite: 135].
   * *Solution:* Integrated accessibility requirements directly into the main Home screen search flow[cite: 135, 141].

2. **Honest Physical Accessibility & "Gulf of Evaluation":**
   * *Problem:* Guests with physical impairments face barriers in historic heritage sites[cite: 135].
   * *Solution:* Clear physical warnings (e.g., spiral staircases vs. ground-floor villas) and visual proof (doorway dimensions in cm) to prevent crises on-site[cite: 135, 140].

3. **Transparent Upfront Pricing:**
   * *Problem:* Commercial platforms employ dark patterns and "dripping pricing" (hidden fees revealed late)[cite: 135].
   * *Solution:* Upfront total costs on all room cards to build trust[cite: 135].

## 🛠️ Requirements Framework (MoSCoW)
* **Must Have:** Upfront transparent pricing, Accessibility filters, Physical constraint warnings[cite: 135].
* **Should Have:** Celebratory confirmation screen (Peak-End Rule), In-app dining add-ons[cite: 135].
* **Won't Have:** Focus B features (Tabletop event booking – declared out of scope)[cite: 135].
